Questions before you start?

No. Complete beginners are welcome. Lessons can start from the very basics.

Yes. Uzbek Pro Academy currently focuses on personalized private lessons.

Lessons can include speaking, vocabulary, pronunciation, grammar and practical everyday Uzbek depending on your goals.

Can I learn Uzbek for a specific purpose?

Absolutely. Lessons can be adapted for travel, family communication, relationships, cultural connection or personal interest.
