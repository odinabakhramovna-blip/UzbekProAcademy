FAQ

Before you message.

A few quick answers to common questions.

Do I need to know Uzbek before starting?

No. Complete beginners are welcome. Lessons can start from the basics and gradually build your speaking confidence.

Are the lessons 1-on-1?

Yes. Uzbek Pro Academy currently focuses on personalized 1-on-1 Uzbek lessons with an instructor.

Who are the lessons for?

Lessons are designed primarily for English-speaking adults who want to learn Uzbek for travel, family, relationships, culture or personal interest.

Can lessons be personalized?

Yes. Lesson content can be adapted to your level, goals, interests and preferred pace.

How do I book a lesson?

Visit the Bookings page to see the available lesson options and continue with the booking process.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Absolutely. Send a message through this page or email Odina directly.
