FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Do I need to know Uzbek before starting?

No. Complete beginners are welcome. Your lessons start from your current level.

Are lessons 1-on-1?

Yes. Lessons are personalized around your goals, level and interests.

Where do lessons take place?

Online. Your booking confirmation will provide the meeting details.

How do I book my first lesson?

Choose a convenient time through the Uzbek Trial Lesson booking page.

Is Uzbek difficult to learn?

Uzbek becomes much easier when you focus on useful patterns, pronunciation and communication instead of trying to memorize everything at once.
